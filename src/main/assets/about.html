Milepost Docent is an Android application that automates docent annoucement for a heritage railway such as living history museums operating historic steam and diesel trains. A GPS track is captured that corresponds to a train scheduled track, and mileposts numbers are associated with the GPS track. The app automatically computes the train's milepost based on its location on the expected track. Pre-recorded docent audio segments are then programmed to play along at specific mileposts as the train progresses on its journey.
